The technique

Nano brows > microblading

Nano Brows create ultra-fine, realistic hair strokes for a beautifully natural, elevated result. Each stroke is placed with a machine instead of a manual blade, and mapped to follow the direction your natural hairs grow.

Carly continues to train in the technique, including with Christina of @browsmade, and opens a limited number of nano spaces so each client gets the time the work deserves.
