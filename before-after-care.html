Before & After Care

Beautiful, healed brows begin before your appointment. Here’s how to prepare for your permanent makeup service and care for your brows while they heal.

Before your appointmentAfter your appointment
01 / Preparation

Before your appointment

For powder, nano, and combo brows, follow these guidelines ahead of your appointment. If Carly has given you individual instructions, follow those instead.

24 hours before

  • Avoid alcohol, coffee, and energy drinks.
  • Avoid vitamin E and fish oil supplements unless your healthcare professional advises otherwise. Do not stop prescribed medication without medical guidance.
  • Wash your hair before your appointment, since you'll need to keep your brows dry during the initial healing period.

Plan ahead

  • Complete waxing, threading, or tweezing at least 48 hours before treatment.
  • Allow at least five days after electrolysis.
  • Avoid AHA preparations on the area for at least two weeks.
  • Allow at least three weeks after chemical or laser peels, Retin-A use on the area, or Botox treatment; check timing with Carly if unsure.
Important: Permanent makeup cannot be performed during pregnancy or while nursing under Carly's current policy. Let Carly know about skin sensitivities, recent procedures, medications, or any concerns before your appointment.
02 / Healing

After your appointment

Fresh brows will look darker and more defined at first. Carly's guidance is that the color generally softens by about 30–50% as the area heals. Some redness, swelling, or sensitivity can occur.

Keep the area clean & protected

  • Avoid direct water contact with the healing brow area; keep it dry and clean.
  • Do not touch the area with your fingers or pick, scratch, or rub any flaking or crusting.
  • Starting on day three, apply a thin layer of vitamin A&D ointment twice daily until healed, using a fresh, clean cotton swab rather than your fingertips, unless Carly has instructed otherwise.
  • Before bathing, Carly's existing instructions recommend a light coating of vitamin A&D ointment applied with a clean cotton swab.

For the first 7–10 days

  • Avoid makeup on the area, brow or lash tinting, soap on the treated area, and direct sun exposure.
  • Avoid saunas, hot tubs, swimming pools, and natural bodies of water until healed.
  • Avoid activities that expose the area to dirt or contamination, including gardening and close animal contact.
  • Consider a clean satin pillowcase while healing.
After healing: Protect your brows from sun exposure and avoid AHA products, including glycolic and lactic acids, directly on the treated area, as these may contribute to fading. Touch-ups are part of the permanent makeup process.
03 / Questions

When to reach out

If you develop itching, swelling, blistering, or another reaction to an aftercare product, stop using it and contact Carly. If you notice excessive redness, worsening swelling or tenderness, red streaks, or other signs of infection, seek prompt medical attention. Do not wait for a reply from the studio if you need medical care.

Healing varies by person. These are general instructions for brow permanent makeup; follow any personalized guidance Carly provides at your appointment.
