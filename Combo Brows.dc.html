Depth, not uniformity

Brows are meant to have depth, not uniformity. Carly thinks of each brow in three sections, and the gradual change in saturation is what makes permanent makeup look polished instead of harsh.

  1. Front

    Soft and airy

    Hair strokes create a natural transition from the bridge of the nose.

  2. Arch

    Slightly more definition

    Strokes and soft shading meet to create shape.

  3. Tail

    The most structured

    Shading completes the brow with fullness and definition.

Combo brows may be for you if…

  • 01You want texture up front and fullness through the tail.
  • 02You like a defined brow but still want it to look like hair.
  • 03Your brows are sparse in some areas and full in others.
  • 04You want to spend less time filling in your brows.
