Your appointment

How it works

Four steps from first conversation to healed brows. Permanent makeup is a two-step process, so your touch-up is part of the plan from day one.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Free · 15 min video, or $50 in person

    Talk through your goals, skin and any previous brow work. Carly recommends the technique that will heal best on you.

  2. 02

    Mapping & shade

    Before any pigment goes in

    Your brows are mapped to your bone structure, natural growth and muscle movement. Pigment is matched to your skin tone, undertone and brow hair.

  3. 03

    Your appointment

    About 2 hours

    Numbing cream is used throughout, and you approve the shape before Carly begins.

  4. 04

    Heal & touch up

    Touch-up 1 hr, from 4 weeks

    Brows soften 30–50% as they heal. Your touch-up refines the healed result.

Before you book

Read the Before & After Care guide

Powder brows give a soft, makeup-inspired finish. Nano brows are fine machine hair strokes for the most natural look. Combo brows pair strokes with shading for texture and density. If you can’t decide, book a free 15-minute virtual consultation.

Numbing cream is used throughout. Sensitive clients may still feel slight discomfort, and the skin can be a little red or swollen afterward.

Color is darker and sharper immediately after the procedure and softens by 30–50% as it heals. Permanent makeup is a two-step process, so your touch-up refines the healed result.

Skip alcohol, coffee and energy drinks for 24 hours before. Wax, thread or tweeze at least 48 hours before, and wash your hair that morning. Procedures can’t be performed while pregnant or nursing. The full list is in the Before & After Care guide.

A non-refundable deposit of $100–$200 is charged at booking, depending on the service. Afterpay is available. Cancellations within 72 hours forfeit the deposit, and same-day cancellations or no-shows are charged the full service.

Yes. Saline removal lightens or removes older brow work. The first appointment is an hour, and follow-up sessions are free.
