What is micropigmentation?

Micropigmentation is a cosmetic procedure that involves the implantation of pigments into the skin, typically to enhance features like eyebrows, eyeliner, or lips.

It can also be used for scalp pigmentation to create the appearance of hair or to cover scars. The process is similar to tattooing but is often more subtle and focuses on achieving a natural look.

Micropigmentation can last several years, depending on factors like skin type, aftercare, and sun exposure. It’s popular for those looking to save time on makeup application or for those with sparse features.
